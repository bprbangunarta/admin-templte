FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you buy.

Do I need a build tool or framework?
No. Mutiara is plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. There is no npm install, no bundler and no framework — open any page in a browser and it works. All vendor libraries are self-hosted and version-pinned.
Can I use it in commercial projects?
Yes. The Personal license covers one end product, the Team license covers unlimited products for up to ten developers, and the Extended license adds SaaS and resale rights.
Does it support dark mode?
Absolutely. Theming is token-based, so light and dark are both first-class and every component — including charts and maps — adapts automatically. The visitor's choice is remembered.
What's included in updates?
New components, pages, bug fixes and improvements. Personal includes 6 months, Team 12 months, and Extended gets lifetime updates.
Is support available?
Yes — all licenses include email support, and Team and Extended plans get priority responses. We're happy to help you integrate and customize.
